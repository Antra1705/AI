## Uncertainty and safety fallback

A window is flagged *uncertain* when confidence < 0.983 or mutual information > 0.0086
(2.5th / 97.5th percentiles of correctly classified validation windows), or novelty
(squared Mahalanobis distance to the nearest class) > 62.9 (99th percentile of validation).
